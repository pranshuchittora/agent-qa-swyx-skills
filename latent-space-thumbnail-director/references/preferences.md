# Learned Thumbnail Preferences

- Sell one specific reason to click: a consequence, decision, contrast, question or capability. Use one dominant headline, normally two to six words; avoid vague labels and shouty all-caps. A concise role, company, verified milestone or genuine guest-post excerpt may help, but do not invent claims or use subcopy to rescue a weak hook.
- Match the episode and current channel context rather than treating FDE examples as universal. White text with an accent on a dark base is common, not a substitute for an episode-specific visual. Use the official LS mark; add FDE only for that series.
- Preserve recognizable people and requested company coverage; identify generated likenesses. Keep faces, text and logos clear of YouTube’s duration badge (approximately the rightmost 18% and bottom 14% at 1280 × 720). Separate dark clothing from dark backgrounds.
- Make alternatives genuinely different in concept and layout, not just color. Check full size and 320 × 180: the idea, faces and headline must remain clear.
